Statement

Six short films about people at the end of a long shift.

I shoot small: short crews, long takes, and as much on film as the budget will carry. The work is mostly concerned with work — the hours that never make it into anyone's story, and what people say when they are too tired to perform.

Everything below is available to watch. Festival programmers and press can find kits and screening dates on the press page.
